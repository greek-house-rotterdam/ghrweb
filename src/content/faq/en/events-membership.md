question: How do I take part in events? Do I need to be a member?
answer: Most events, such as theater performances or talks, are open to everyone.
  However, certain events, like the Vasilopita cutting, are open only to members of
  the Association of Greeks in the Netherlands. You can find all our events on the
  “Events” page.
