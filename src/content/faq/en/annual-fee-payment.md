question: How and when do I pay the annual membership fee?
answer: New members pay the annual fee upon completing their application, once they
  receive a confirmation email with a payment link. Current members pay the annual
  fee by the end of the year via direct transfer to the Association of Greeks in the
  Netherlands' bank account. You can find the membership rates on the “Become a Member”
  page.
