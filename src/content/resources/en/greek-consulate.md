The Greek House in Rotterdam, in collaboration with the Embassy of Greece in the Netherlands, hosted Consul Konstantinos Kalambokidis for a discussion on:

- The distinction between the Embassy and the Consulate (Consular Office)
- Greeks in the Netherlands
- Acquiring Dutch citizenship
- Digital services of the Consular Office
- Apostille stamps

[Watch the discussion on YouTube](https://youtu.be/-ZAAicGxkk8).

The discussion was moderated by Konstantina Patsiogianni and Thodoris Klouvas. Filming took place at The Greek House in Rotterdam.
