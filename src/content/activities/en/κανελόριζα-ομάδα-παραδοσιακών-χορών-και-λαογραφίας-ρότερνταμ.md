Active in traditional dance and folklore since 2011, the Kaneloriza group continues its journey from 2026 under the roof of The Greek House in Rotterdam. In our weekly classes, traditional dances from every corner of Greece are taught step by step—from the islands and Asia Minor to Epirus, Macedonia, and Crete—highlighting the rich folklore of each region.

Classes are open to both beginners and experienced dancers, creating a vibrant community where tradition comes alive and brings people together.
