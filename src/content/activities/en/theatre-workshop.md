The first exclusively Greek-speaking stage in the Netherlands! The theatre group “Parakmi” was formed in 2022: a close-knit group turning dreams into roles, inviting you to express yourself, act, and discover your inner actor.

It all starts with a script and turns into theatre. Come discover how written words transform into a live performance! Together, we will explore, study, and create, while you’ll also have the opportunity to take part in professional artistic projects.
