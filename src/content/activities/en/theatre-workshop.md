The first exclusively Greek-speaking stage in the Netherlands! The theatre group “Παρακμή” was formed in November 2021. A close-knit group that turns dreams into roles, inviting you to express yourself, act, and discover the actor within you.

It starts with a script and becomes theatre. Join us to discover how a written piece transforms into a live performance! Together we will study, explore, and create, while you'll also have the opportunity to take part in professional artistic projects.
