These classes focus on photography as a medium for personal expression and creative exploration. The goal is to develop a personal vision, understand images as a form of storytelling, and gradually build your own body of work. Through photo discussions, analyzing works by prominent photographers, and creative exercises, participants are invited to connect with their own experiences and translate them into images.
