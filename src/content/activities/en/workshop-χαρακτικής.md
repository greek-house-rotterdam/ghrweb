In our workshops, our goal is to create and discover the art of printmaking together. Each workshop features a different theme, and the sessions are tailored for beginners. No prior experience is needed—just bring your imagination and enthusiasm! In this workshop, you'll learn the basics of the linocut technique, finding out how to carve, ink, and print your very own unique artwork. Our aim is to give participants the opportunity to explore a new craft and develop their own creative expression!
