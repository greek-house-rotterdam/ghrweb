If a typical afternoon finds you surrounded by books, if you love reading, discovering new viewpoints, and discussing different perspectives, then come join our Book Club!

We meet every 2nd and 4th Friday of the month to discuss our favorite books, explore themes and writing styles, and share ideas. Stay connected with reading in Greek and make the most of our lending library!
