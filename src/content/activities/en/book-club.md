If a typical afternoon finds you surrounded by books, if you love reading, discovering new ideas, and discussing different perspectives, then join our Book Club.

We meet every 2nd and 4th Friday of the month to chat about our favourite books, themes we discover in our reading, writing styles, and so much more. Stay connected with reading in Greek and make the most of our lending library!
