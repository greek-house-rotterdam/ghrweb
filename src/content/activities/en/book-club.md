If a typical afternoon finds you surrounded by books, if you love reading, discovering new viewpoints, and discussing different perspectives, then join our Book Club!

We meet every 2nd and 4th Friday of the month on the upper floor / library of The Greek House to talk about favourite books, themes we explore while reading, writing styles, and so much more. Keep in touch with reading in Greek and make the most of our lending library!

Here is the full schedule and themes for the year:

\- 09/10/26 Meet & Greet

\- 23/10/26 Classics

\- 13/11/26 Horror

\- 27/11/26 Drama / Death

\- 11/12/26 Local Dialects & Vernacular

\- 08/01/27 Poetry

\- 22/01/27 Psychology

\- 12/02/27 Love Stories

\- 26/02/27 Comics

\- 12/03/27 History / Biographies / Memoirs

\- 26/03/27 Politics

\- 09/04/27 Fantasy

\- 23/04/27 Humour

\- 14/05/27 Mystery

\- 28/05/27 Overrated / Overhyped Books

\- 11/06/27 Spirituality

\- 25/06/27 Books Adapted for Film or TV


Activity coordinator: Tzortzina Gerothanasi
