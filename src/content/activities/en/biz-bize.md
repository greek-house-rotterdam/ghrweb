Biz Bizé at the Greek House: we're opening our doors!

Every Saturday for two hours, from 14:00 to 16:00, the members of the Greek House look forward to catching up and getting to know you over a warm cup of coffee or tea.

Come by to learn more about the Association of Greeks in the Netherlands, the activities we organise, how you can become a member, and how to get involved and contribute to everything we're creating together!
