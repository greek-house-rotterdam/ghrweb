The Greek House in Rotterdam (*Het Griekse Huis in Rotterdam*) is the cultural group of the Association of Greeks in the Netherlands. It is known for a wide range of artistic activities—and so much more.

It began its journey in the autumn of 2017. Some of its founding members include Thodoris A. Klouvas, Fani Traikou, Michalis Kemanetzis, Thodoris Karypidis, Dafni Berikaki, and others.

![The hall and kafenio of The Greek House](/images/eeo-koffiehuis.jpg)

## Our goals and vision

The main goal of The Greek House is to continuously bring new activities to life and support projects by creative Greek artists and professionals. We aim to establish *The Greek House in Rotterdam* as a vibrant home for arts, literature, dialogue, and entertainment.

Our long-term vision is to keep the Association of Greeks in the Netherlands active, engaging, and welcoming—for everyone who visits us, whether to take part in our activities or simply to enjoy a cup of coffee together.

Do you have a creative project in mind, or would you like to volunteer for the activities of The Greek House? [Get in touch with us](/gr/contact) or drop by The Greek House during our opening hours so we can get to know each other!
