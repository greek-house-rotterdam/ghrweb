The Greek House in Rotterdam (*Het Griekse Huis in Rotterdam*) is the cultural group of the Association of Greeks in the Netherlands. It is known for a wide range of artistic — and not only — activities.

It began its journey in the autumn of 2017. Among its founding members are Thodoris A. Klouvas, Fani Traikou, Michalis Kemanetzis, Thodoris Karypidis, Dafni Berikaki, and others.

![The hall and cafe of the Greek House](/images/eeo-koffiehuis.jpg)

Do you have a creative spark and/or would you like to volunteer for the activities of the Greek House? Then [get in touch with us](/gr/contact) or drop by the Greek House during opening hours so we can get to know each other!
