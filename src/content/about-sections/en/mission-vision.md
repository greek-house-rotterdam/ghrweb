## Our mission

The Greek House in Rotterdam was created to bring people together, foster a sense of community, and create opportunities for meaningful involvement through volunteering and culture.

Through our activities, events, and initiatives, we aim to provide a welcoming space where people can meet, share ideas, develop new skills, and actively contribute to something greater than themselves. Always with deep respect for the history of our Association.

We believe that the strength of The Greek House lies in its people. In the volunteers who dedicate their time and knowledge, in the groups collaborating together, in ideas brought to life, and in the relationships forged through shared effort.

Our mission is to keep this community vibrant, open, and creative, giving everyone the opportunity to participate, contribute, and leave their own mark.

## Our vision

We envision The Greek House as a point of reference for the Greek community: a lively meeting place for creativity and collaboration, where people feel a sense of belonging and know their contribution matters.

We want to cultivate a strong, active volunteer community where initiatives are encouraged, ideas have room to grow, and collaboration forms the foundation of everything we do.

We aspire to evolve continuously, creating new opportunities for participation and broadening our impact, both within the Greek community and across Dutch society at large.

Our goal is not just to organize events and activities, but to create the environment where people can connect, find inspiration, and build something lasting and meaningful together.

We want The Greek House to be more than just a space or an organization. We want it to be a living community shaped by its people, growing through what they create together, and providing a true sense of belonging for the Greek diaspora in the Netherlands.

## Our values

- **Community & Participation:** We cultivate an environment where everyone feels welcome and has the opportunity to participate actively in the life of the Association.
- **Volunteering & Giving Back:** We recognize that every volunteer's time, skills, and dedication are a precious part of our shared effort—perhaps the most precious of all.
- **Collaboration & Mutual Support:** We believe we achieve more when we work as a team, share responsibilities, and support one another.
- **Initiative & Creativity:** We encourage fresh ideas, creative thinking, and initiatives that enrich our activities and strengthen our community.
- **Equal Participation & Transparency:** We welcome diverse ideas and groups, striving for fair treatment, clear processes, and open communication.
- **Growth & Outward Looking:** We stay open to new collaborations, people, and perspectives, striving to evolve and strengthen our ties with the wider society, responding to the needs we all share today.

## Putting our vision into practice

Our vision is not just about what we want to achieve in the future. It also shapes how we operate every single day.

- **Giving room to ideas:** We encourage volunteers to propose new initiatives and take the lead.
- **Working as one team:** We maintain clear roles and responsibilities, with the shared goal of advancing the entire Association.
- **Communicating openly:** We share information, coordinate our efforts, and ensure everyone has access to what they need.
- **Supporting all initiatives equally:** We recognize the contribution of every group and strive to highlight the full spectrum of our activities.
- **Evolving together:** We build on our experience, learn from our initiatives, and continuously improve how we collaborate.

The Greek House in Rotterdam is its people. And its future is shaped by everything we dare to envision and create together.
