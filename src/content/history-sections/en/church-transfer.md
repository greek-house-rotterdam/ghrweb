## Decades of social contribution

A key goal of the Association has always been to promote good relations with other social groups. This was achieved through church bazaars, an annual ecumenical service on the Sunday of Orthodoxy, lectures, and musical evenings. The highlight of the year was the annual gala, attended by around 500 people from leading businesses in Rotterdam.

The Association always operated with a small number of members, but this never dampened its energy. For 53 years, it maintained the Holy Church of Saint Nicholas — the only Greek Orthodox church in the Netherlands until 1987 — as well as supporting the priest, for whom it also provided a residence.

## The transfer

As fundraising events became less successful due to the economic crisis and donations ceased for the same reason, the Association could no longer cover the church's maintenance costs.

On **26 November 2010**, through a deed of donation, the church was transferred to the Holy Metropolis of Belgium and Exarchate of the Netherlands and Luxembourg. While the Association held the property rights to the church, it had always been under the religious jurisdiction of the Metropolis.
