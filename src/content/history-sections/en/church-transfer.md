## Decades of community contribution

A key goal of the Association was always fostering good relations with other communities. This was achieved through church bazaars, an annual ecumenical service on the Sunday of Orthodoxy, lectures, and musical evenings. The highlight of the year was the annual gala ball, attended by around 500 guests from prominent Rotterdam businesses.

The Association always operated with a modest number of members, but that never diminished its drive. For 53 whole years, it maintained the Church of Saint Nicholas—the only Greek Orthodox church in the Netherlands until 1987—and supported the priest, for whom it also provided a home.

## The transfer

As fundraising events struggled amid the financial crisis and donations ceased for the same reason, the Association was no longer able to shoulder the church’s maintenance costs.

On **26 November 2010**, by deed of donation, the property was transferred to the Holy Metropolis of Belgium and Exarchate of the Low Countries and Luxembourg. While the church building had been owned by the Association, ecclesiastically it had always fallen under the jurisdiction of the Metropolis.
