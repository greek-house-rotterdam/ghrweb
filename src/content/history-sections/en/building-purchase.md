*The Association's building since 1961, at Van Vollenhovenstraat 18.*

On **20 September 1961**, the Association purchased the three-storey building at Van Vollenhovenstraat 18, very close to the church.

It housed the Association's offices and also served as the residence for the parish priest. For several decades now, the ground floor has served as a gathering space for members of the Association and parishioners.

![The entrance of the building, with the Greek flag and the Association's sign](/images/eeo-building-1961.jpg)

*The entrance of the building, with the Greek flag and the Association's sign.*

## The school moves (1965–1971)

Between 1965 and 1971, the gathering space was converted into a school, welcoming the first teacher seconded by the Greek state at the request of the Board of Directors.
