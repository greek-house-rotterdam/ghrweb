![The Association's building since 1961, at Van Vollenhovenstraat 18](/images/eeo-building-1961.jpg)

On **September 20, 1961**, the Association purchased the three-story building at Van Vollenhovenstraat 18, located very close to the church.

The building housed our offices and also served as the residence for the priest. For several decades, the ground floor has been a community space for our members and parishioners.

## The school moves in (1965–1971)

The community space was transformed into a school during the period 1965–1971, with the arrival of the first teacher seconded by the Greek state at the request of the board of directors.
