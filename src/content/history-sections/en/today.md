Today, the Association’s mission continues through the cultural group *The Greek House in Rotterdam*. Founded in 2017, it organizes events, classes, and cultural activities for the Greek community in the Netherlands.

Following a partial renovation in 2022 and the restoration of its facade in 2024, the Greek House remains the heart of our community.

In **2026**, we celebrate 80 years since the Association was founded — eight decades of Greek culture and community in the Netherlands.
